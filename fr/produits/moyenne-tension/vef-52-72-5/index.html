Aperçu

VEF | 52 | 72,5 | Outdoor Voltage Transformer, Head-Type, single pole insulated. The outdoor voltage transformers are insulated with cast resin. Primary winding and core with secondary winding are cast in one operation with cycloaliphatic epoxy resin prepared under vacuum (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are housed in a robust terminal box made of cast aluminium, which can be sealed in the case of revenue metering purpose. Primary terminals are optionally inserts, bolts, terminal pads or clamp type connectors. The mounting position is upright. The transformer can be earthed via the existing earthing screw or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.

Caractéristiques

  • Détection de défaut à la terre
  • Transformateur de mesure MT
  • Prise secondaire
  • Température ambiante −5 … +40 °C
