Aperçu

The voltage transformer is earthed via the earthing screw identified by the earth symbol.

VEF 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 | Outdoor Voltage Transformer, single pole insulated. The voltage transformer is cast resin insulated for outdoor use.

Primary windings and core with secondary winding(s) are molded in one operation with vacuum-treated cycloaliphatic epoxy resin (Full encapsulation).The secondary terminals are housed in a robust terminal box of cast aluminum which can be sealable in case of revenue metering purpose. As primary connection are either with Screw terminals, Connecting bolts, Flat Terminals or Bolt Terminals for cable clamps provided.

Installation in upright position. The voltage transformer is earthed via the earthing screw identified by the earth symbol.

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.
