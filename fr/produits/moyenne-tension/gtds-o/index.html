Aperçu

GTDS(O) 10 | 20 | 30 Bushing Type Current Transformer

On request, both ends of the primary passage can be provided with standard cast resin rail covers for the passage of two or three bars 80 x 10 mm or 100 x 10 mm or three bars 120 x 10 mm. Upon request, panels for other profiles can also be manufactured. The cut-outs of the panels have a tolerance of 1 to 2 mm in relation to the primary conductor. Supporting the bars is also essential here. Secondary terminals up to 8 M6 can be placed in the terminal box. Each terminal can be grounded directly, independently of the connection screw. From the terminal box three PG 16 cable bushings are provided to led out the secondary cables. As an Option a second terminal box is available

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.
