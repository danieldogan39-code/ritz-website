Aperçu

GSZ 10 / 20 Indoor Supply Voltage Transformer, Big-Type, DIN 42600, part 3, two-pole insulated. Primary winding and core with secondary winding(s) are cast with epoxy resin in a single operation (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are located in a terminal box which can be sealed in the case of revenue metering purpose. The mounting position is optional.

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.

Caractéristiques

  • Isolation bipolaire
  • Intérieur
  • Prise secondaire
  • Température ambiante −5 … +40 °C
