Aperçu

GTDS(O)FI 10 | 20 | 30 Outdoor indoor bushing type current transformer On request, both ends of the primary duct can be fitted with standard cast resin rail covers for the passage of two or three rails 80 x 10 mm or 100 x 10 mm or three rails 120 x 10 mm. Upon request, panels for other profiles can also be manufactured. The cut-outs of the panels have a tolerance of 1 to 2 mm in relation to the primary conductor. Supporting the rail is also mandatory here. Up to 8 M6 secondary terminals can be integrated in the terminal box. Each terminal can be grounded directly, independently of the connection screw. 3 PG 16 cable bushings are provided for the secondary cables to be led out. The terminal box meets the requirements of IP44 / IP65 (on request).

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.
