Aperçu

GSW 10 | 20 | 30 Support Type Current Transformer, Big Type. The terminal box can insert 6 secondary terminals, whereby a maximum of 3 cores are available in a CT. On request the CT can be provided with a capacitive divider, which offers the option of a voltage display via a glow lamp by an additional CK terminal clamp. The terminal box consists of aluminium alloy and has a transparent cover. The terminals are nickel plated and of metric size M6.

Special Designs:

Tropicalized Design,

Rated Frequency 60 Hz,

Primary Reconnectable 1:2:4,

Secondary Taps for several primary currents,

Standards of other countries,

Primary Permanent Current > 1,0

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.
