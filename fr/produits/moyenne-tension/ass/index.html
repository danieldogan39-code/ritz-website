Aperçu

Support-type current transformer, block design, narrow type to DIN 42600 part 8; available as size 1, 2, 3

ASS 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 | 40,5 Support-Type Current Transformer Block-Type Transformers; narrow design DIN 42600, part 8; The current transformers are fully encapsulated in brown epoxy resin, i.e. high voltage winding, secondary winding(s) and core(s) are encapsulated in one process. For a voltage display or simple voltage measurement, a capacitive measuring layer can be provided, which is connected to a terminal in the terminal box. - Primarily reconnectable transformers are always supplied in parallel (for the high current). The mounting position is as needed. Grounding: The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.
