Aperçu

High-current transformer, support type

GSS0 10 | 17,5 | 20 High Current CT

Support Current Transformer. The type GSSO is a single-wire current transformer.The basic version is supplied with a free round passage. The primary busbars must be passed through by the customer and supported in front of and behind the transformer. For the measuring accuracy it is irrelevant with the available free space, if the busbars are not centrically passed through. In the primary opening there is a metallic shielding tube with a potential connection. This must be fixed with screws to the primary conductor by the customer. On request, the current transformer can be equipped with busbar covers for centric passage through the busbars. The busbar guide has a tolerance of 1 to 2 mm in relation to the primary conductor. Here, too, it is essential to support the busbar.

Les données produits sont disponibles en anglais et en allemand.
