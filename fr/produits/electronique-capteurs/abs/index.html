Aperçu

Combined sensor, support type, for air-insulated switchgear

ABS 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 | The block combi sensor is equipped as standard with double shielded cable and coaxial plug with gold plated contact pins for the voltage signals of the current and voltage measurement. If a current signal is required for current measurement, a current carrying cable is used for rejection. The coupling electrode for voltage indication is routed on the right side next to the fully shielded secondary clamping area. Both the current (with integrated load) and the voltage output can be operated permanently open or short-circuited. Conforms to standard: IEC 61869-10/-11
