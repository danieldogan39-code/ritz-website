Aperçu

ZKSW 60...ZKSW 300 Series, Indoor Interposing Current Transformer,

possible design options:

ZKSW 60, Size 1, Primary Current 1…20A, External dimensions (W/H/L): 60 / 73 / 30 mm

ZKSW 60, Size 2, Primary Current 1…20A, External dimensions (W/H/L): 60 / 78 / 52 mm

ZKSW 70, Primary Current 1…20A, External dimensions (W/H/L): 70 / 86 / 52 mm

ZKSW 100, Primary Current 1…20A, External dimensions (W/H/L): 100 / 118 / 73 mm

ZKSW 300, Primary Current 1…20A, External dimensions (W/H/L): 100 / 151 / 73 mm

Standard accessories: 2 foot fasteners and a transparent terminal cover.

Mounting in any position.

Application

1. At lower operating currents, when no direct measurement is desired (electrical isolation of the measuring circuit, keeping the operating voltage away, measuring instruments nominal current not equal to the operating current).

2. for connection to main transformer to the following purposes:

a) To reduce the conduction losses of long measuring lines: Reduce from e.g. 5 to 1 A at the installation location of the main transformer and, if necessary, increase from 1 to 5 A at the measurement location.

b) To protect sensitive equipment against overcurrents by means of a small overcurrent limiting factor with the most accurate selection of the actually required rated burden.

c) For matching the secondary current of the main transformer to the rated current of the equipment to be connected.
