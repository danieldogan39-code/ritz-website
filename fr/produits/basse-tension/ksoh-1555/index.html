(E) KSOH 1555 Indoor single-phase window type current transformers for indoor application.

possible design options:

KSOH 1555 C, height 170, 110 and 65 mm up to max. 6 or 12 connecting cables fabric hose DIN 57281 HO7V-K1X2.5, length according to order (PO)

KSOH 1555 H, height 285, 170, 110 and 65 mm,

- Version with max. 6 or 12 connection cables DIN 57281 HO7V-K1X4, length according to order (PO), braided steel tubing, length according to order (PO), shrink tubing, woven tubing

- Version with base plate and outlets up to max. 6 or 12 connection cables

- Version with base plate and terminal box
