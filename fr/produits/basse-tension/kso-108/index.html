Aperçu

KSO 108 Indoor: Window Type Current Transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

possible model options

Inside-Opening; 24 mm for bus bars up to 1x80x10 mm or for round cables up to Ø 24 mm

Size W/H/D: 100 / 126 / 62 mm;

Special designs:

Um 1.2 kV Accessories:

fixing device for primary conductor

transparent terminal cover

4 Foot mountings
