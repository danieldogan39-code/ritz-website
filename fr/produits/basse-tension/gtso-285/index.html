GTSO 285 Window Type Current Transformer for indoor Application.

possible model options:

with rectangular aperture for primary conductor

(AxB): 42 x 32 mm ... 122 x 112 … 162 x 52 mm

For bars up to 3x 160 x 10 (4x 120x 10)mm,

or with round aperture for primary cable Inner Ø 45.5…171 mm for cable up to Ø 170 mm

W / H / D: 285 / 285 mm (variable) *

(size 1, 2, 3 or 4) (*incl. terminal cover)

Um 1.2 kV

Primary conductor fixing

2 Mounting feet,

transparent terminal cover
