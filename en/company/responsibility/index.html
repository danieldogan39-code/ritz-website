Society

Business success makes social commitment possible: through the RITZ Foundation and at our sites.

Owned by a foundation

Liselotte Ritz established the RITZ Foundation in 1986 to support Alzheimer’s research. Today the foundation owns the RITZ Group.

For more than three decades it has supported the Institute of Molecular Neurogenetics at University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE). In May 2025, UKE researchers presented their latest findings at our Hamburg headquarters, including a project funded by the foundation.

Governance

Clear rules for ourselves and our supply chain, and a protected way to report violations.

ZVEI-VDMA Code of Conduct

Our code of conduct applies to all companies of the RITZ Group. It is based on the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, the OECD Guidelines, the ILO core labour standards and the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights.

  • Preventing corruption
  • Fair competition
  • Human rights
  • Health and safety
  • Environmental and climate protection
Document

Code of Conduct for Suppliers

We expect our suppliers to follow the same principles, including in their own supply chains.

  • Zero tolerance of corruption
  • No child or forced labour
  • Fair wages and working hours
  • Occupational safety
  • Conflict minerals
Document

Conflict-free purchasing policy

We do not buy raw materials or components that finance armed groups in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Our relevant suppliers have confirmed, to the best of their knowledge, that they supply only conflict-free materials.

  • Tin
  • Tungsten
  • Tantalum
  • Gold
Document

Authorised Economic Operator (AEO-F)

Since 2015 RITZ has been certified by the Hamburg-Stadt main customs office as an Authorised Economic Operator, for customs simplifications and security in the supply chain.

Document

Report violations confidentially

Employees and third parties can report suspected violations, such as corruption, fraud, discrimination, harassment, or breaches of human rights or environmental rules.

  • Manager or contact personTalk to your own manager or your contact person at RITZ.
  • Legal departmentThe General Counsel at the Hamburg headquarters.
  • External ombudspersonA lawyer in Frankfurt am Main receives reports confidentially, in German and English and free of charge. As a rule, he does not disclose your identity to RITZ.
