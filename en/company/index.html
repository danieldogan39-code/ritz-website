About RITZ

Since 1945 the RITZ name has stood for instrument transformers from Hamburg. Today more than 1,500 employees at ten locations in Europe, China and the USA develop and manufacture solutions for electrical power engineering.

Our vision

Electricity is the most important form of energy of the future. We keep developing and are becoming a leading, profitable specialist and provider of solutions for electrical power engineering worldwide.

With our global manufacturing footprint and matching sales network we support customers worldwide from within their region – with high standards of quality and service.

RITZ and the RITZ Foundation stand for safety, reliability and responsibility across generations. People are at the centre of everything we do.

History

From an instrument transformer factory in Hamburg to an international group.

  1. 2026

    New high-voltage plant

    Opening of the production and test centre for high-voltage transformers in Waynesboro, Georgia (USA).

  2. 2024

    Growth

    First year with more than 200 million euros expected sales. Decision to build a new high-voltage plant in Waynesboro.

  3. 2022

    High voltage in the USA

    The RITZ Group increases sales to 150 million euros; high-voltage production resumes in Waynesboro.

  4. 2020

    Focus on instrument transformers

    Divestment of the power transformers division.

  5. 2018

    Reorganisation

    Transformer production in Wirges is distributed to RITZ locations worldwide.

  6. 2015

    Kecskemét

    New factory building in Hungary; first activities for a new high-voltage division.

  7. 2008

    RITZ USA

    RITZ is established in Hartwell (USA) with medium- and low-voltage transformers.

  8. 2006

    High voltage

    Sale of the former high-voltage division to AREVA.

  9. 2003

    Shanghai

    RITZ Shanghai produces in its own factory.

  10. 1999

    MWB Bamberg

    Acquisition of the medium-voltage division – and with it the solid-insulated busbars – of Messwandler-Bau Bamberg.

  11. 1996

    Hungary and China

    First steps towards production in Hungary and China.

  12. 1994

    DUROMER

    Acquisition of DUROMER – the portfolio grows with cast resin products.

  13. 1991

    TuR Dresden

    Acquisition of the instrument transformer division of Transformatoren- und Röntgenwerk Dresden; high-voltage production in Waynesboro.

  14. 1989

    WTW Wirges

    Acquisition of Wandler- und Transformatorenwerk Wirges.

  15. 1986

    RITZ Foundation

    Liselotte Ritz establishes the RITZ Foundation to support research on Alzheimer’s disease.

    Illustration ©UKE

  16. 1956

    Austria

    RITZ is established in Marchtrenk.

  17. 1945

    Foundation

    Dr Hans Ritz founds RITZ Messwandler in Hamburg.

Group structure

The RITZ Foundation owns the RITZ Group. RITZ unites long-established instrument transformer brands: MWB, WTW and TuR.

RITZ Foundation

For more than three decades the RITZ Foundation has supported Alzheimer’s research at the Institute of Molecular Neurogenetics at University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE). Researchers from the UKE regularly present their latest findings at RITZ.
