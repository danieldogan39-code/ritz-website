Overview

TGBZI / TGBZA 12 | 24 | 36 | 12 - 36 kV metal-clad indoor voltage transformer, supply transformer, touch-proof. The active part consists of a leg core with primary and secondary winding. The potential connections of the housing to earth are made via the foot mounting. The high-voltage connection is designed as an inner cone (TGBZI) or outer cone (TGBZA) device connection in accordance with EN 50181, suitable for cable plug A, B, C or sizes 1 and 2. The secondary connection is made via the terminal box. Connect the cable lugs of the cables to the corresponding terminals with the screws, contact washers and washers.

Plug-in size Inside Cone:

Size 1 for 600A, Ith 20 kA/1s

Size 2 for 800A, Ith 20 kA/1s

Plug-in size Outside Cone:

Size A for max. 250A, 12.5 kA/1s

Size B for max. 400 A, 16 kA/1s

Size C for max. 600 A, 28 kA/1s

Features

  • Double-pole insulated
  • Indoor
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Touch-proof
