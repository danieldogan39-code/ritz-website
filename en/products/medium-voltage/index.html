Medium-voltage instrument transformers

Current, voltage and combined transformers from 3.6 to 72.5 kV for indoor and outdoor use – in standard designs to DIN 42600 and as special versions.

Product finder

Instrument transformers supply measuring instruments, meters and protection relays and insulate them from live parts of the installation. RITZ medium-voltage transformers are cast resin insulated – outdoor versions use cycloaliphatic epoxy resin.

In addition to support and block-type transformers, the range includes touch-proof metal-clad transformers, railway and generator transformers, and broadband transformers for power quality measurement.
