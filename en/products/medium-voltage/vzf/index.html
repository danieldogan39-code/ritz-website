Overview

VZF 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 | Outdoor voltage transformer, double-pole insulated.

The outdoor voltage transformers are insulated with cast resin. Primary winding and core with secondary winding are cast in one operation with cycloaliphatic epoxy resin prepared under vacuum (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are housed in a robust terminal box made of cast aluminium, which can be sealed in the case of revenue metering purpose. Primary terminals are optionally inserts, bolts, terminal pads or clamp type connectors. The mounting position is upright. The transformer can be earthed via the existing earthing screw or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Features

  • Double-pole insulated
  • MV metering transformer
  • Outdoor
  • Secondary tap
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
