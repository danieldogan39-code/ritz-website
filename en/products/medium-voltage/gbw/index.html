Overview

Metal-clad current transformer, 12–36 kV, touch-proof

GBW 12 | 24 | 36 metal-clad current transformer for indoor applications, touch-proof. Construction: The active part and the primary connection cone are encapsulated with epoxy resin in a single operation. The transformer is completely enclosed in an earthed metal housing. The potential connections of the housing to earth are made via the foot mounting. The high-voltage connection is realized as a Inner sealing cone for cable-plug connection acc. to DIN 47637-I-36-630 (size 1 / Ø 95 mm, size 2 / Ø 102 mm).
