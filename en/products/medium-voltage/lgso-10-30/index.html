Overview

LGSO 10…30 Indoor window-type current transformer-Generator Transformer

It is installed in air duct tubes by means of threaded socket connections on the outer surface of the transformers. The secondary connections are located on the outer surface of the transformer. After mounting the transformer in the air duct tube, the secondary connections are located in an encapsulated terminal box which is located on the tube.

Features

  • Cast resin insulation
  • Insulation class E (120 °C)
  • For use on generators
  • Earthing terminal
  • Indoor
  • Mounting in any position
  • MV metering transformer
  • MV protection transformer
  • Extended overload capability 120 %
  • Secondary tap
  • Single-phase
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Suitable for tropical climates
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
