Overview

IGD 36 Indoor Bushing Type Current Transformer Bar-Type

For large rated currents, this transformer with its slightly larger diameter serves as a supplement to the bar-type current transformer range. Several primary rated currents can be realized by means of one or more secondary taps for other rated current ratios. Each secondary terminal has the possibility of direct screw-through grounding. On delivery, the first terminal of each core is visibly connected to earth. The terminal box offers space for 6 secondary terminals, so that a maximum of three cores can be supplied in one transformer. The mounting position is optional.

Features

  • Insulation class E (120 °C)
  • Earthing terminal
  • Indoor
  • Multi-core design
  • MV metering transformer
  • MV protection transformer
  • Extended overload capability 120 %
  • Secondary tap
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Suitable for tropical climates
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
