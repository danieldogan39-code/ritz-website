Overview

Bushing-type current transformer with fixed conductor

GDS 10…36 For large rated currents, this transformer with its slightly larger diameter serves as a supplement to the bar-type current transformer range. Several primary rated currents can be realized by means of one or more secondary taps for other rated current ratios (GDS 10…36). Each secondary terminal has the possibility of direct screw-through grounding. On delivery, the first terminal of each core is visibly connected to earth. The terminal box offers space for 6 secondary terminals, so that a maximum of three cores can be supplied in one transformer. The mounting position is variable.

Features

  • Insulation class E (120 °C)
  • Earthing terminal
  • Indoor
  • MV metering transformer
  • MV protection transformer
  • Extended overload capability 120 %
  • Secondary tap
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Suitable for tropical climates
