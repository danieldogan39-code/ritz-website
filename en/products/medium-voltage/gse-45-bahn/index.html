Overview

GSE 45 | 52 | Indoor Voltage Transformer, single pole insulated.

The terminal box has a sealable, transparent cover made of Makrolon. It offers space for 6 secondary terminals with the possibility of direct screw-through earthing. Information about minimum distances between the phases and against earthed system parts cannot be made generally binding, since these depend on the installation conditions. The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Special designs:

For Tropical Climates

secondary reconnected

Railway applications

For use on electric rail vehicles. Insulation made of weather-resistant cycloaliphatic epoxy resin; all outdoor parts made of corrosion-resistant materials, maintenance-free. Compliant with EN 50463 and the TSI LOC&PAS requirements, and therefore suitable for on-board energy metering. Manufactured in an IRIS-certified plant.
