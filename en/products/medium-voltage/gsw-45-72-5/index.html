Overview

GSW 45 and 72,5 (52 - 72,5 kV) Indoor current transformers Support design are cast resin insulated.

The current transformer has a base plate or base track. The base plate of the current transformer is made of an aluminium alloy and the base track is made of galvanised steel. All primary connections are made of nickel-plated copper. Other materials or connection types on customer request.

Type D: direct connection.

Type H: horizontal flat connection with one hole (up to 600 A) or two holes (up to 1500 A).

Type V: vertical flat terminal, perpendicular to the secondary terminals, with one hole (up to 600 A) or two holes (up to 1500 A).

Type F: vertical, flat terminal, parallel to the secondary terminals, with one hole (up) up to 600 A) or two holes (up to 1500 A).

The terminal box is made of an aluminium alloy and has a transparent cover. The secondary terminals are nickel-plated and fitted with M6 screws.

Features
