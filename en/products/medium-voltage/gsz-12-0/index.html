Overview

Voltage transformer, double-pole, small type to DIN 42600 part 7

GSZ 12/0 | 3,6 | 7,2 | 12 | Indoor Voltage Transformers, double-pole insulated.

Primary winding and core with secondary winding(s) are cast with epoxy resin in one operation (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are housed in a terminal box, which can be sealed in the case of revenue metering purpose. The mounting position is variable.

Features

  • Double-pole insulated
  • Indoor
  • MV metering transformer
  • Secondary tap
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
