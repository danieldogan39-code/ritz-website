Overview

GSWS 12/24D 12 | 24 Support current transformers, Block-Type; narrow design DIN 42600, part 8; indoor application. The current transformers are fully encapsulated in brown epoxy resin, i.e. high voltage winding, secondary winding(s) and core(s) are encapsulated in one process. For a voltage display or simple voltage measurement, a capacitive measuring layer can be provided, which is connected to a terminal in the terminal box. - Primary reconnected CTs are always supplied in parallel reconnection (for the high current strength). Any mounting position is possible. Grounding: The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Features

  • For air-insulated switchgear
  • Capacitive tap / CK terminal / capacitive divider
  • Insulation class E (120 °C)
  • Earthing terminal
  • Indoor
  • MV metering transformer
  • MV protection transformer
  • Extended overload capability 120 %
  • Primary reconnectable
  • Secondary tap
  • Silicone-free
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
