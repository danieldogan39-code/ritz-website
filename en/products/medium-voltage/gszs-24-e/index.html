Overview

GSZS 24 | 12…24 kV Indoor self-supply voltage transformer, narrow design, DIN 42600, part 9, double pole insulated. Primary winding and core with secondary winding are cast with epoxy resin in one operation (full casting). The secondary terminals are housed in a terminal box. The mounting position is optional.

Features

  • Double-pole insulated
  • Indoor
  • Secondary tap
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
