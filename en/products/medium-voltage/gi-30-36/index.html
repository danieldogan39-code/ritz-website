Overview

GI 30 | 36 | Support current transformers, block transformers for indoor applications, the current transformers are fully encapsulated in brown epoxy resin, i.e. high voltage winding, secondary winding(s) and core(s) are encapsulated in one process. For a voltage display or simple voltage measurement, a capacitive measuring layer can be provided, which is connected to a clamp in the terminal box. - Mounting in any position is possible. Grounding: The transformer can be grounded via the grounding screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the grounded frame of the switchgear.

Features

  • Cast resin insulation
  • Insulation class E (120 °C)
  • Earthing terminal
  • Indoor
  • Mounting in any position
  • MV metering transformer
  • MV protection transformer
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Suitable for tropical climates
  • TuR design
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
  • Vibration-proof
