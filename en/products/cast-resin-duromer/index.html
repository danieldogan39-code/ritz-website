DUROMER cast resin parts

Our subsidiary DUROMER develops cast resin systems and manufactures insulating parts and solid-insulated devices for electrical engineering.

Product finder

DUROMER formulates casting compounds – mainly polyurethane-based – for low and medium voltage and electronics. Our engineers advise on material and process selection, moulded part design and tooling – independently of resin manufacturers.

Every system is tested and documented in our own laboratory: insulation capability, freedom from partial discharge and climatic requirements. DUROMER has been ISO 9001 certified since 1998 and supplies customers including Siemens, Philips and Megger.

What are duromers?

Thermosets, also called duromers, are plastics that cure through a chemical reaction into a dense molecular network. Curing is irreversible: duromers have no softening range, remain strong and dimensionally stable at high temperatures and show favourable fire behaviour.
