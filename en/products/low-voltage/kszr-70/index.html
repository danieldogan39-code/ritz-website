Overview

KSZR 70 | 0.72 kV Low Voltage Indoor Voltage Transformer, Double-pole insulated.

Dimensions W/H/L: 70 / 94 / 52 mm

Weight: approx. 0.6 kg

Standard accessories:

1 transparent terminal cover

2 mounting feet

Optional accessories:

Twistable snap-on mounting bracket for mounting rail (DIN EN 60715-TH35)

Mounting in any position. The voltage transformer is maintenance-free

Features

  • Double-pole insulated
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
