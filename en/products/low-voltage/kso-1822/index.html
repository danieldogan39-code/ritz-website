Overview

KSO 1822 Indoor: Window Type Current Transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

possible model options

Inside-Opening; 121 mm for bus bars up to 3x120x10 (3x100x10) mm or for round cables up to Ø 83 mm

Size W/H/D: 185 / 209 / 62 mm;

Special designs:

Um 1.2 kV Accessories:

fixing device for primary conductor

transparent terminal cover

4 Foot mountings

Features

  • Indoor
  • LV metering transformer
  • Mounting in any position
  • Single-phase
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
