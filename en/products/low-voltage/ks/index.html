Overview

(E) KS Indoor Window-Type Current Transformer, Blue Series; standard series for Indoor use.

possible design options:

(E) KS 50 - 02: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 20,3 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 50…600A, for Bus Bars up to 20x12 mm or cables up to Ø 20 mm

(E) KS 60 - 03: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 25,3 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 50…750A, for Bus Bars up to 30x12 mm or cables up to Ø 25 mm

(E) KS 70 - 04: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 30,4 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 50…1000A, for Bus Bars up to 40x13 (30x20)mm or cables up to Ø 30 mm

(E) KS 79 - 05: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 41 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 100…1250A, for Bus Bars up to 1x 50x15 (2x 40x10)mm or cables up to Ø 41 mm

(E) KS 95 - 06: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 51 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 300…1600A, for Bus Bars up to 2x 60x12 mm or cables up to Ø51 mm

(E) KS 120 - 08: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 61 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 500…3000A, for Bus Bars up to 2x 80x12 mm or cables up to Ø 61 mm

(E) KS 160 - 12: Size 1, 2, 3, Inner Ø 101 mm, depth: 30, 40, 50 mm, Primary Current: 1000…3000A, for Bus Bars up to 2x 120x12 (3x 100x12)mm or cables up to Ø 101 mm
