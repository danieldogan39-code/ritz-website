Overview

KSO 86 Indoor: Window Type Current Transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

possible model options

Features

  • Indoor
  • LV metering transformer
  • Mounting in any position
  • Single-phase
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
