Overview

ZKSW 60, indoor intermediate current transformer, winding current transformer low voltage.

possible model variants:

size 1: W/H/D: 60 / 81 / 30 mm; Weight: 0,3 kg

Size 2: W/H/D: 60 / 81 / 52 mm; Weight: 0,5 kg

standard accessories:

2 foot fixings,

transparent terminal cover

Special accessories:

twistable snap-on mounting for mounting rail (DIN EN 60715-TH35)

Mounting in any position.

Features

  • Indoor
  • Mounting in any position
  • Single-phase
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
