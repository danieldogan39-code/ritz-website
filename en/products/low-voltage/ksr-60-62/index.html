Overview

The KSR 60 / KSR 62 series are tube-type current transformers with a secondary winding on a toroidal core. The active part is completely enclosed by two ultrasonically welded polycarbonate housing half-shells. The transformers are supplied with a removable primary conductor in the form of a copper tube.

possible model versions:

Inner Ø 50 mm for bolts M12 up to Ø 16 mm

Size 1: W / H / L: 60 / 81 / 30 mm

Size 2: W / H / L: 60 / 86 / 52 mm

Size 3: W / H / L: 50 / 70 / 69 mm

Weight: 0.6 kg

Standard accessories:

2 mounting feet,

transparent terminal cover

Optional accessories:

rotatable snap-on fixing for mounting rail (DIN EN 60715-TH35)

Mounting in any position

The current transformer is maintenance-free
