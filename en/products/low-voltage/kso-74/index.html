Overview

(E) KSO 74 Indoor slip-over current transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

(E) KSS 74 busbar current transformer with primary conductor. possible design options:

(E) KSO 74, inner diameter 40.2 mm without busbar, for busbars up to 40 x 12 (35 x 13; 30 x 18)mm or round cables up to Ø 30 mm

(E) KSS 74, inner diameter 40.2 mm with rail 40 x 12 mm,

Size in each case: W/H/D: 70 / 94 / 52 mm;

Special designs:

Um = 1.2 kV

Tropical design

Cast resin casting with tropical strength

Accessories: common foot mounting (for mounting as 3-phase unit)

Snap-on mounting according to DIN EN 50022

Cable mounting Accessories for the voltage path

Optional accessories: 

Twistable snap-on mounting bracket for mounting rail (DIN EN 60715-TH35) 
