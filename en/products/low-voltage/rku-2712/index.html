Overview

RKU 2712 Split-Core Current Transformer for indoor Application.

Standard accessories:

transparent terminal cover,

2 foot mounting feet

Inner Ø 125 mm for cables up to Ø 125 mm

W / H / D: 270 / 295 / 122 or 182 mm * (size 1 or 2)

(*incl. terminal cover)

Weight: Does vary

Optional accessories:

1 wall bracket (Fixing Device)

Mounting in any position

The current transformer is maintenance-free

Features

  • Cast resin insulation
  • Insulation class E (120 °C)
  • Earth-fault detection
  • Indoor
  • LV metering transformer
  • LV protection transformer
  • Single-phase
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
