Overview

(E) KSOHO 3411 Indoor single-phase window current transformers for indoor application.

possible design options:

KSOHO 3411, height 214, 170, 110 and 65 mm,

up to max. 6 or 12 connecting cables in the Hose

DIN 57281 HO7V-K1X4, length 5,00m

Features

  • Indoor
  • LV metering transformer
  • Single-phase
  • Ambient temperature −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaration of conformity / verifiable
