Overview

UGSS 105 Split-core type Current transformers also for earth fault detection for 12…36 kV for indoor application.

For cables up to Ø 50 mm.

Rated Primary Currents: 40…80 … 1250 A,

Rated Secondary Currents: 1A or 5A.

For indoor application, two core halves are cast with the secondary winding in casting resin. The core interfaces are lapped, creating a perfect magnetic connection. For reliable detection of the magnetic field of the small residual earth-fault active currents, the secondary winding is arranged on both halves of the transformer and connected together at the factory.

UGSS 105; Split-core type current transformer, also for earth fault detection, for 12…36 kV.

These split-core ct’s are used to detect small earth currents. The retrofitting can be carried out retrospectively around already installed cables with fitted terminations. All three conductors of the cable are enclosed together. The earth wire is fed back on the cable within the transformer aperture. Due to the cable insulation, the transformer is independent of the operating voltage (application mainly for Um 12 kV to Um 36 kV).
