Overview

KSER 70 | 0.72 | 1.2 | kV Low voltage indoor voltage transformer, single pole.

Dimensions W/H/L: 100 / 118 / 70 mm

Weight: approx. 1.1 kg

Standard accessories:

2 transparent terminal cover

Snap-on fastener on top hat rail acc. to DIN EN 60715-TH 35

Possible special versions:

Slider for Snap-on mounting bracket ET 4720-1-2

different rated voltage 1.2 kV on request

differing rated frequencies

Tropical version

Filling with cast resin;
