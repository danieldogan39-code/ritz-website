Low-voltage instrument transformers

Current and voltage transformers for metering and protection up to 3.6 kV – RITZ is one of the leading European manufacturers of low-voltage transformers.

Product finder

The range extends from window-type current transformers for switch cabinets and split-core transformers for retrofitting to laboratory, summation and interposing transformers. Many types are verifiable and therefore suitable for revenue metering.

Most are manufactured at the Marchtrenk plant in Austria, an approved verification office of the Federal Office of Metrology and Surveying (BEV). Official verification in the factory saves time, freight and packaging.
