SIS busbars

The fully insulated SIS busbar system transmits high currents in tight spaces – an alternative to parallel cables and metal-enclosed bus ducts.

Product finder

The busbars are capacitively graded and concentrically earthed: touch-proof, short-circuit proof and naturally cooled. They are manufactured and routine-tested at the Kirchaich plant.

For every order we recalculate the support spacing – from short-circuit forces, busbar diameter, phase spacing, mass and bending stiffness – so that no mechanical resonances occur at system frequency and its harmonics.

up to 40.5 kVrated voltage SIS
up to 8,000 Arated current SIS
up to 72.5 kV, 5,000 ASISD bushings

Advantages

Design

Space-saving; enclosed space can be planned smaller; very small bending radii and almost any geometry (depending on material).

Installation

Low effort thanks to standardised mounting and fixing parts; little project engineering required from the customer.

Safety

Capacitively graded field distribution, high thermal and dynamic short-circuit strength, phase-to-phase faults practically impossible.

Operation

Natural cooling through adequately sized conductors, high operational reliability through routine testing in the factory.

Technical details

Production lengths

Lengths and geometries depend on production facilities, site conditions and means of transport.

Multi-part connections

Long connections are made of sections; an insulated sleeve covers the graded busbar ends and ensures continuous, potential-free routing.

Fixing

Modular system of aluminium double-C profiles, angles, hammer-head bolts and plastic clamps; fixed and sliding bearings according to the support spacing calculation.

Earthing

An earthing bar sized for the short-circuit current runs parallel to the busbars; busbars, sleeves and profiles are connected to it.

Connections

Standardised flat terminals with flexible connectors; project-specific terminals on request. With fixed terminals at both ends, a sleeve compensates tolerances and thermal expansion.
