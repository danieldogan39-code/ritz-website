Overview

Passive low-power voltage transformer

MGTK 12/24 Voltage sensor, compact ohmic voltage divider with overvoltage protection. Corresponds to standard: IEC 60044-7 (in future IEC 61869-11). Due to their compact design, the voltage sensors are ideally suited for the first installation or retrofitting of local network stations or switchgear. The voltage sensor is screwed instead of the sealing plug into the cable output at the rear of the T-connector. For use of different cable connection Fittings: From Manufacturers, such as Nexans, Cellpack, Südkabel, Prysmian ... The cone of the voltage sensor corresponds to EN50181 Type C1.
