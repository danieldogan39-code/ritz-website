Overview

Passive low-power voltage transformer, outdoor

GSER(F) 52 Outdoor Low-Power Passive Voltage Transformer, consists of a high voltage resistive divider (R1, R2), which transforms the primary voltage Up to a law voltage Us, which can be processed by the secondary system. An electromagnetic shield ensures that the voltage sensor can also be used in environments with strong interference influences. Since there is no potential separation between the primary and secondary sides, a surge arrester protects the downstream devices. Conforms to standard IEC 61869-11.
