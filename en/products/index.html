Products

Instrument transformers convert currents and voltages proportionally and in phase into measurable, standardised values. They supply meters, measuring instruments and protection relays and insulate them from high voltage.

RITZ supplies standard types and realises special requirements – from design to customer specification through to routine testing in the factory.
