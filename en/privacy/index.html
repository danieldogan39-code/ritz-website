Privacy notice

We only process personal data to the extent necessary to operate this website and to handle your inquiries.

1. Controller

RITZ Instrument Transformers GmbH, Wandsbeker Zollstraße 92–98, 22041 Hamburg, Germany, info@ritz-international.com. You can reach our data protection officer, Mr Peter Christian Felst, at datenschutz@ritz-international.com or at our postal address marked “Data Protection Officer”.

2. Visiting the website

When you visit the website, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and system information) in server logs in order to deliver the website and ensure its security (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Logs are deleted after 14 days at the latest.

3. No cookies, no tracking

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or marketing tools. Fonts, maps and all other content are loaded from our own server; no data is transferred to Google or other third parties. Your settings (appearance, inquiry list, comparison) are stored only locally in your browser; they leave your device only when you send an inquiry.

4. Inquiries and contact form

When you send us an inquiry, we process your details (company, name, email, optionally phone and country, message, selected products) to handle it and for follow-up questions (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR). The data goes to the responsible sales team within the RITZ Group and, where applicable, to the responsible representative in your country. We delete it once the inquiry has been fully processed and no statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Applications

Vacancies link to our career portal at Personio (Personio SE & Co. KG, Munich). The portal’s privacy notice applies to processing there. We process applications sent by email to carry out the application procedure (Section 26 BDSG, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR).

6. External links

Links to map services (directions), the career portal or Microsoft Teams open third-party services only after you click them. The respective provider is responsible for processing there.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

8. Automated decisions

No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.
