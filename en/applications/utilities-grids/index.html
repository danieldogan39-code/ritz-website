Utilities and grids

Distribution and transmission system operators need transformers that measure accurately for decades – for billing, protection and power quality monitoring.

What matters

  • Revenue metering with verifiable transformers and official verification
  • Protection cores with suitable accuracy limit factors, also multi-core
  • Compact substations with little space for metering panels
  • Correct loading when electronic meters are used

RITZ supplies medium-voltage transformers for indoor and outdoor use, combined transformers and high-voltage transformers up to 550 kV. Official verification takes place at the state-recognised testing authority in the Dresden plant; in Austria at the approved BEV verification office in the Marchtrenk plant.

Metal-clad combined transformers replace an air-insulated metering panel: according to our example calculation this saves around 12,000 euros and building space at 20 kV – with a service life of at least 30 years and no cleaning intervals.
