Renewables, HVDC and power quality

Power electronics and renewable generation are changing grids: DC components, harmonics and stabilisation systems require new measurement technology.

What matters

  • High-precision measurement of DC, AC and mixed currents
  • Recording harmonics into the kHz range
  • Measurement in SVC installations, back-to-back stations and DC links

EMVI electronic current transformers work on the zero-flux principle; the compact EMVI 4005 measures currents up to 12,000 A. EGIW and EVBA electronic voltage transformers measure DC and AC voltages with isolation or buffer amplifier and optical transmission.

For power quality we supply current, voltage and combined transformers as broadband transformers with optionally extended frequency range up to 9 kHz.
