Industry and infrastructure

Industrial networks have special requirements: very high currents, DC components, hazardous areas and little space for retrofitting.

What matters

  • High currents between transformer and switchgear in tight spaces
  • Retrofitting without disconnecting cables
  • DC and mixed currents in electrochemistry and electrolysis
  • Explosion-protected versions and mining

Solid-insulated SIS busbars up to 8,000 A, high-current transformers, split-core transformers also for earth-fault detection, explosion-protected support-type current transformers and electronic transformers for direct currents up to 24 kA.
