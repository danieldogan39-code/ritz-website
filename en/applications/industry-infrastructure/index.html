What matters

  • High currents in a confined space

    Solid-insulated SIS busbars up to 8,000 A – a compact alternative to parallel cables and metal-enclosed busbars.

    1SIS busbars

  • Retrofitting without disconnecting cables

    Split-core cable-type current transformers are mounted around the cable; versions for earth-fault detection are also available.

    2Split-core current transformers

  • Large loads such as arc furnaces

    High-current transformers for medium voltage up to 24 kV and 4,000 A.

    3High-current transformers

  • DC and mixed currents

    EMVI electronic current transformers work on the zero-flux principle and measure DC, AC and mixed currents; the compact EMVI 4005 up to 12,000 A.

    4DC measurement

  • Hazardous areas

    Explosion-proof support-type current transformers for hazardous areas and mining.

    5Explosion protection
