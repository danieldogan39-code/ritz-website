Metering and testing

Wherever energy is billed or calibrated, accuracy decides – and so does the proof of it.

What matters

  • Official verification without detours
  • Laboratory transformers with several measuring ranges
  • Safety when the secondary circuit is open

The Dresden plant houses an independent, state-recognised testing authority entitled to officially verify instrument transformers. The test bay in Austria is an approved BEV verification office; the laboratory is accredited to ISO/IEC 17025.

The EOSS 122 open-circuit voltage protection limits dangerous voltages if the secondary circuit of a current transformer is accidentally opened.
