What matters

  • Verification without detours

    In the Dresden plant, an independent, state-recognised testing authority officially verifies instrument transformers; in Marchtrenk, the BEV-approved verification office carries out verification for Austria.

    1Verifiable transformers

  • Accuracy with proof

    Our calibration laboratory in Marchtrenk is accredited to ISO/IEC 17025 and determines the ratio error with an uncertainty as low as 0.010 %.

    1Verifiable transformers

  • Laboratory transformers with several ranges

    The KSW 300 and LKS 104 laboratory and multi-range transformers cover several measuring ranges with one device.

    2Laboratory and multi-range transformers

  • Safety with an open secondary circuit

    The EOSS 122 open-circuit protection limits the voltage across an open secondary circuit to below 50 V. It is built directly into current transformers with a 1 A secondary current.
