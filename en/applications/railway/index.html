Railway

On board and in traction power supply, robustness, weight and approval count. RITZ railway transformers are manufactured in an IRIS-certified plant.

What matters

  • On-board energy metering to EN 50463 and TSI LOC&PAS
  • Insulation voltages up to 40.5 kV on the vehicle roof
  • Protection against damage and in the unlikely event of bursting
  • 16.7 Hz traction current in stationary installations

The GSEFB outdoor voltage transformer works as a roof-mounted high-voltage transformer on electric rail vehicles; the GSEFBC is additionally embedded in a silicone composite insulator and fitted with a pressure relief cap. The GSO(F)B window-type current transformer serves as a line or return current transformer. A preliminary EC intermediate statement of verification can be issued for many transformers.

In 2026 RITZ Marchtrenk achieved the IRIS Silver Quality Performance Level. Of around 3,000 IRIS-certified companies worldwide, only 401 have reached this status so far.
