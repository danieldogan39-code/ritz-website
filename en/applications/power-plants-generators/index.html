Power plants and generators

Very high currents flow at the generator with small phase spacings – external fields must not distort the measurement.

What matters

  • Currents up to 50,000 A with small phase spacing
  • High ambient temperatures in generator leads
  • Auxiliary supply for secondary systems directly from the grid

The IRBT generator current transformer has been part of the range for many years. Dedicated compensating windings cancel external-field effects. The revised design is rated for ambient temperatures up to 80 °C, is around 15 percent lower and, depending on the design, 5 to 10 percent lighter.
