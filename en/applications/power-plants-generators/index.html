What matters

  • Very high currents at narrow phase spacings

    Generator transformers such as the IRBT for currents up to 50,000 A. Targeted compensation windings cancel out disturbing external fields.

    1Generator transformers

  • High ambient temperatures

    The revised IRBT design is rated for up to 80 °C, around 15 percent lower and, depending on the design, 5 to 10 percent lighter; a terminal box makes the secondary connection easier.

    1Generator transformers

  • Auxiliary supply for secondary systems

    Auxiliary supply voltage transformers provide the power for protection and control equipment directly from the medium-voltage grid.

    2Auxiliary supply transformers

  • High-voltage grid connection

    High-voltage transformers up to 550 kV, made in the USA.

    3High-voltage transformers
