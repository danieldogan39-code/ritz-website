Additional burden

Este artículo solo está disponible en inglés.

An additional burden is an additional device that is used to achieve sufficient burdening of measuringtransformers that have not been sufficiently burdened (e.g. when using electronic meters).

Note: Measuring transformers that have not been burdened do not comply with the accuracy class.
