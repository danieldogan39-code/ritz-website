Resumen

Support-type current transformer, block design, large type to DIN 42600 part 5

ASN 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 Support current transformer. The terminal box provides space for 6 secondary terminals, so that a maximum of 3 cores can be supplied in one transformer. On request, the transformers can be equipped with a capacitive divider, which offers the possibility of voltage indication via a glow lamp via an additional Ck clamp in the terminal box. Primary switching and secondary tapping are possible. Special versions: Tropical version, rated frequency 60 Hz, primary switchable 1:2:4, secondary taps for various primary rated currents, standards of other countries, primary continuous current > 1.0 Ipn, CK terminal for voltage displays with surge arrester.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.
