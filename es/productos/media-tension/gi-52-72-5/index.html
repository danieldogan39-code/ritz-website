Resumen

Support-type current transformer to DIN 42600 part 1

GI 52 | 72,5 Supporting current transformers are insulated with cast resin. Primary winding and core(s) with secondary winding are encapsulated in a single operation with resin prepared under vacuum (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are housed in a robust terminal box made of cast aluminium, which can be sealed in the case of revenue measuring. The primary connections are either sockets fixed in cast resin, connection bolts, flat connections or cable clamps. Insulating mould material: Only cycloaliphatic epoxy resin is used as insulating mould material.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.
