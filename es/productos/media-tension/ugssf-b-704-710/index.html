Resumen

UGSSF(B) Outdoor cable split core current transformer - traction current transformer, for use on locomotives and Traction railcars. The two core parts are joined together by tightening four special clamping screws. As a result of the cable insulation, the transformer is independent of the operating voltage. The devices are mainly used in systems up to 36 kV.

Aplicaciones ferroviarias

For use on electric rail vehicles. Insulation made of weather-resistant cycloaliphatic epoxy resin; all outdoor parts made of corrosion-resistant materials, maintenance-free. Compliant with EN 50463 and the TSI LOC&PAS requirements, and therefore suitable for on-board energy metering. Manufactured in an IRIS-certified plant.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.
