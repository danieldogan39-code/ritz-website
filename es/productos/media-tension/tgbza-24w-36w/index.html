Resumen

TGBZA 24…36W / 24 - 36 kV metal-encapsulated indoor voltage transformer, supply transformer, double-pole insulated, touch-proof. The indoor voltage transformers are fully encapsulated in epoxy resin. The transformer is completely enclosed in a grounded metal housing. The high-voltage connection is designed as outer cone (TGBZA) device connection in accordance with EN 50181, suitable for cable plug B or C. The secondary connection is made via the terminal box.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

Características

  • Aislamiento bipolar
  • Interior
  • Temperatura ambiente −5 … +40 °C
  • A prueba de contacto
