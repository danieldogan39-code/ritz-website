Resumen

GS 12 | 24 | 36 Block Type Current Transformer. The current transformers are fully encapsulated in brown epoxy resin, i.e. high voltage winding, secondary winding(s) and core(s) are encapsulated in one process. For a voltage display or simple voltage measurement, a capacitive measuring layer can be provided, which is connected to a terminal in the terminal box. - Any mounting position is possible. Grounding: The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

  • Aislamiento de resina colada
  • Clase de aislamiento E (120 °C)

  • Temperatura ambiente −5 … +40 °C

  • Resistente a vibraciones
