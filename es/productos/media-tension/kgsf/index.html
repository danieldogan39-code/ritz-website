Resumen

KGSF 12…72.5. The single-phase combined instrument transformer combines one current and one voltage transformer in one housing. The secondary connections are designed as M6 single terminals and can be earthed to the mounting rail. The secondary terminals are protected by a sealable cover. Protection class IP44, optionally IP 55 - Single-phase combination transformer Um =12…72.5 kV - According to standard VDE, IEC/DIN EN 61869-4,- for outdoor applications.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

Características

  • Ejecución de varios núcleos
  • Transformador de medida MT
  • Exterior
  • Temperatura ambiente −5 … +40 °C
