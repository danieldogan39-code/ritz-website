Resumen

Voltage transformer, double-pole, large type to DIN 42600 part 3

VZN 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 | Indoor voltage transformers, double pole insulated.

Primary winding and core with secondary winding(s) are cast with epoxy resin in one operation (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are placed in a terminal box which can be sealed in the case of revenue metering purpose. The mounting position is variable.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

Características

  • Aislamiento bipolar
  • Interior
  • Transformador de medida MT
  • Toma en el secundario
  • Temperatura ambiente −5 … +40 °C
  • Declaración de conformidad / verificable
