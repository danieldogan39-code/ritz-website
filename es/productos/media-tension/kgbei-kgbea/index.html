Resumen

Metal-clad combined instrument transformer, 12–36 kV

KGBEI / KGBEA 12 | 24 | 36 | 12 - 36 kV Metal-clad indoor combi-transformer with inductive single-pole voltage and current transformer section, touch-proof design. The active part consists of a leg core with primary and secondary winding. The potential connections of the housing to earth are made via the foot mounting. The high-voltage connection is designed as an Inside Cone (KGBEI) or an Outside Cone (KGBEA) device connection in accordance with EN

50181, suitable for Cable Connection Plug A, B, C or sizes 1 and 2. The secondary connection is made via the terminal box. Connect the cable lugs of the cables to the corresponding terminals using the screws, contact washers and washers.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

Características

  • Detección de faltas a tierra
  • Interior
  • Con envolvente metálica
