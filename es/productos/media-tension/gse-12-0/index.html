Resumen

Voltage transformer, single-pole, small type to DIN 42600 part 7

GSE 12/0 |Indoor voltage transformers, single phase, small type, DIN 42600, part 7.

The secondary terminal box has a sealable, transparent cover made of Makrolon. It can be installed in any position. Information on minimum distances between the phases and between earthed system parts cannot be made generally, as these depend on the installation conditions. The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Possible special designs:

Tropical version

secondary switchable

primary fuse

Primary connection cable cast-on

silicone-free

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.
