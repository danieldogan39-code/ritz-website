Resumen

GSWFK 10…72,5 (12 - 72,5 kV) Outdoor Cascade Current Transformer. The current transformer works according to the principle of the inductive transformer. The secondary side is galvanically insulated from the primary side. The current transformer complies with all transformer-specific national and international standards. The current transformer is used exclusively to transform a high current into a lower, measurable current. The current transformer must be mounted on a plane surface. The terminal box meets the requirements of protection class IP44 (optional IP55). The primary conductor is routed centrally through the upper stage (GSOF) (not included in delivery). The current transformer is grounded on the rear of the transformer.

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.
