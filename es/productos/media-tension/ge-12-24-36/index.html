Resumen

GE 12 / 24 / 36 Indoor voltage transformer, single-Phase insulated.

The terminal box has a sealable, transparent cover made of Makrolon. It offers space for 6 secondary terminals with the possibility of direct screw-through earthing. It can be installed in any position. Information about minimum distances between the phases and against earthed system parts cannot be made generally, since these depend on the installation conditions. The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

Special designs:

Tropical version

secondary switchable

primary fuse

Primary connection cable cast-on

silicone-free
