Resumen

UGSS 704 / 710 These split core type current transformers are used to detect low earth active currents. The conversion can be carried out retrospectively around already installed cables with mounted terminations. All three conductors of the cable are enclosed together. The ground wire is fed back to the cable within the transformer aperture. As a result of the cable insulation, the transformer is independent of the operating voltage (application mainly for Um 12 kV to Um 36 kV).

possible model variants:

UGSS 704: Inside Ø 40 mm for cables up to Ø 40 mm

UGSS 710: Inside Ø 105 mm for cables up to Ø 105 mm
