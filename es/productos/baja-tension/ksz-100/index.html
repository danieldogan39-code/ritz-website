Resumen

KSZ 100 | 0.72 | 1.2 | kV Low Voltage, Voltage Transformer, Double-pole insulated.

Dimensions W/H/L: 102 / 105 / 141 mm

Weight: approx. 3 kg

Standard accessories:

2 transparent terminal cover

4 mounting feet

Possible special versions:

different rated voltage 1.2 kV on request

differing rated frequencies

Tropical version

Filling with cast resin;

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

Características

  • Aislamiento bipolar
  • Temperatura ambiente −5 … +40 °C
