Resumen

The KSSH 44 is a bar-type current transformer with a secondary winding on a toroidal core.

The active part is housed in an enclosure insulated with cast resin.

A copper bar serves as the primary conductor, max. current load: Icth = 130 A

The secondary connection is made via 300 mm long leads

Size: W / H / L: 44 / 56 / 30 mm

Weight: 0.3 kg

Application:

Instrument transformer for operational purposes, e.g. for mounting on fuse switch-disconnectors

Mounting in any position

The current transformer is maintenance-free

Los datos de producto están disponibles en inglés y alemán.

Características

  • Interior
  • Transformador de medida BT
  • Montaje en cualquier posición
  • Monofásico
  • Temperatura ambiente −5 … +40 °C
