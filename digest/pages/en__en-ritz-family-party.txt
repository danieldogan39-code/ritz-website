[POSTTITLE] RITZ FAMILY PARTY
[POSTCONTENT] What do a bobby car track, face painting for children and a bouncy castle have to do with RITZ?
 They were all part of this year’s summer party at our Ottendorf-Okrilla site!
 Under the motto “Family joy”, our employees celebrated the inauguration of our newly built dispatch hall together with their families.
 With delicious food and drink, good music and children’s attractions, we would like to say THANK YOU to our employees!
 Thank you very much for your active support and for making the family party so special! It was very nice to spend the day together with you and your family!
 Would you like to be there next time? Then take a quick look at the job portal in our.
 We look forward to receiving your application!
