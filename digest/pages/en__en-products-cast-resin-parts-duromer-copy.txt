[TEXT] Our subsidiary DUROMER specializes in the use of casting resins in the electrical industry.
 DUROMER develops and formulates casting resin molding compounds for electrical applications in the low and medium voltage range and for electronics. Our specialists design and produce cast-resin insulated devices and molded parts for use in electrical power engineering. In the engineering area, you will be advised on the selection of materials and processes as well as on customer-specific designs.
 DUROMER’s main production areas are the manufacture of cast resin compounds and their processing into insulating parts as well as the production of solid insulated electrical devices.
 DUROMER has been ISO 9001 certified since 1998.

[template] What are duromers?
Thermosetting polymers, also referred to as thermosets or duromers, are plastics that harden through a chemical reaction. They usually react by polyaddition and crosslink to form a closely interlinked lattice of macromolecules, which gives the thermoset its high mechanical strength. Since this kind of hardening, or curing, changes the structure of the molecule, it is an irreversible process.
Thermosets have no softening range; the material retains its strength even at high temperatures and has excellent heat resistance. Thermosets are characterised by their hardness and rigidity. Their low coefficient of thermal expansion ensures high dimensional stability. The behaviour of thermosets in fire is also advantageous.

Manufacture of casting resin compounds
We know that the key to a quality product is the special composition of casting resins.
After considering the respective advantages and disadvantages of different casting resin systems, the various components are mixed according to the requirements of the finished part.
DUROMER mainly manufactures on the basis of polyurethanes and acts as a system house partner of Bayer Material Science AG.A general overview of the polyurethane-based casting resin systems produced by DUROMER is available for download here. Development all the way to series production
Our specialist knowledge of casting resin systems is put into practice in the new and further development of moulded parts.
All systems are tested and documented in our laboratory after completion. This includes development tests with regard to insulation capacity, freedom from partial discharge and the fulfilment of climatic requirements with the corresponding supporting documents.
In the production of moulded parts, casting resin machines are used for both vacuum casting and the pressure gelation process.
