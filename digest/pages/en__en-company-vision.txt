[H H2] RITZ - Empowering our electrical future
[TEXT] Experience is what counts. We have been active in the electrical energy technology market with our solutions for over 75 years. We have always been able to adapt to changing market conditions. With our global manufacturing footprint and the corresponding sales network, we can support our customers worldwide from the respective region. We have very high standards of quality and service – this is what the RITZ Group stands for.
 Our vision: 
Electricity is the most important form of energy of the future. We will continue to develop and become a leading and profitable global specialist and provider of electrical energy technology solutions!
 Ritz and the Ritz Foundation stand for safety, reliability and cross-generational responsibility. People are at the centre of everything we do.
