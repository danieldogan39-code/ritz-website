[POSTTITLE] Ritz offers new service for PD measurement of power transformers
[POSTCONTENT] With the help of OMICRON ‘s measuring and analysis systems and high-voltage sources, Ritz will be able to carry out offline partial discharge measurements on power transformers directly at the customer’s premises in future. On request, you can find out up to what power level these measurements are possible on site.
 Read all the information in the article on this topic in the current OMICRON magazine and feel free to contact our colleagues on site at our location in Wirges if you have any further questions.
