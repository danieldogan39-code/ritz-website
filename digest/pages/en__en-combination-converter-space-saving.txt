[POSTTITLE] Combination converter Space saving
[POSTCONTENT] Focus on metal-encapsulated combi converters 
 Cost savings of approx. 12,000€ when using combi-transducers compared to measuring field with cable input field. Cost savings of approx. 12,000€ when using combi converters compared to measuring field with cable input field
 Example:
 Costs for 3 20kV combination transformers = approx. 10.000€ 
 Costs for air-insulated 20kV switchgear with cable entry panel and measuring panel = €22,000 
 Space-saving through use of the cable cellar and therefore no need for additional space in the building and associated construction cost savings.
 Variety of parameters: multi-core and multi-winding designs possible
 Offset counting possible
 Frequency-optimized design up to 9 kHz possible
 very long service life of at least 30 years and therefore sustainable!
 Safe to touch during operation due to earthed housing
 Maintenance-free and insensitive to dirt over the entire service life, in contrast to air-insulated measuring fields, which must be cleaned every 4 years in accordance with DGUV V3 regulations. In this case, the measuring cell must be switched off. With a combi-transformer, cleaning is no longer necessary and saves costs for the energy supplier for decades. 
 There are very few manufacturers of 30kV air-insulated measuring fields on the market. As an alternative, 30kV combination transformers are often used here 
 Mounting position independent
