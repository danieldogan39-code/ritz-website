[POSTTITLE] F1 in schools
[POSTCONTENT] The organization “F1 in schools” is a non-profit organization that aims to get young people interested in technology. To this end, several teams in schools around the world are designing model cars that are powered by compressed air. These teams compete against each other in races. Two teams sponsored by Ritz took part in this year’s final, which was held in Singapore from September 10 to 13. At one point, a team from Germany was sponsored with the name Cardiem. The second team from China took part in the competition under the name Spykar. Cardiem came 15th with 713 points and Spykar 16th with 694.4 points. Cardiem also received the “Digital media award”. The Ritz Group would like to congratulate the teams on their results, and you can see some photos of the competition and our teams below.
