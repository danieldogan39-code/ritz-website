[POSTCONTENT] Ritz Instrument Transformers announces construction of new high voltage manufacturing facility
 Lavonia, Georgia, USA: Ritz Instrument Transformers, Inc. announced today that it will construct a new state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Waynesboro, Georgia, for the production of high voltage instrument transformers. The initial investment is expected to be $28 million and the project will create 130 manufacturing and professional jobs. Construction is scheduled to begin in October 2024.
