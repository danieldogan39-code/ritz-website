Áttekintés

Passive low-power voltage transformer, outdoor

GSR 27 / 38 Voltage sensor, consists of an ohmic voltage divider. On customer request the output voltages can be adapted for special applications. Likewise, the standardized output voltage of the voltage sensor can be calibrated according customer's receivers and can therefore be used universally. Standard IEC 61869-11.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
