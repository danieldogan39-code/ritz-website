Áttekintés

Metal-clad voltage transformer, 12–52 kV, touch-proof

GBEI(N), GBEA(N) 12…52 metal-clad Indoor Voltage transformer, single pole insulated, touch proof.

The primary coil and the iron core together with the secondary winding(s) are completely resin embedded and cast in a single process step. The secondary terminals are integrated in the resin body and protected by an aluminium box. The high voltage end of the primary winding (terminal “A”) can be provided with an outer or inner silicon cone and is applicable for specific plug-in or cable connection according to DIN 47636 part 4. The other end of the primary winding (termi¬nal “N”) is grounded inside of the secondary terminal box [Standard design]. For other secondary design the terminal “N” will be grounded by the manufacturer

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
