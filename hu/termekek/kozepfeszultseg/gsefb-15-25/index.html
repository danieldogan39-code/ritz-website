Áttekintés

GSEFB 15…25 Outdoor voltage transformer, single-pole insulated. The railway voltage transformers are used for insulation voltages up to 40.5 kV, comply with European and international standards and are manufactured in an IRIS certified factory. The transformers conform to EN50463, meet the requirements of the current TSI LOC&PAS and are therefore suitable for energy measurement on the vehicle. For many transformers, a preliminary EC declaration of intermediate conformity can already be issued for this purpose.

Vasúti alkalmazások

For use on electric rail vehicles. Insulation made of weather-resistant cycloaliphatic epoxy resin; all outdoor parts made of corrosion-resistant materials, maintenance-free. Compliant with EN 50463 and the TSI LOC&PAS requirements, and therefore suitable for on-board energy metering. Manufactured in an IRIS-certified plant.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
