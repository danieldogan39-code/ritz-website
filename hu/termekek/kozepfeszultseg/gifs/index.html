Áttekintés

Outdoor support-type current transformer, standard design

GIFS 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 Outdoor Current Transformer Support Type Compact Design are cast resin insulated. Primary winding as well as the core(s) with secondary winding are molded in one single process with epoxy resin, which has been processed under vacuum. The secondary terminals are located in a sturdy terminal box made of cast aluminium. For metering transformers this terminal box can also be sealed. Primary terminals are optionally inserts, or terminal pads. Routine Tests: The routine tests include ratio-error and phase-angle-tests, induced and applied voltage tests and partial discharge tests. These tests are done in accordance with DIN EN 61869-2 (except when other standards have been agreed upon).

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
