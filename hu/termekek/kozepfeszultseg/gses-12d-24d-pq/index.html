Áttekintés

Inductive single-pole voltage transformer with optionally extended frequency range

GSES24D with optional extended frequency range up to 9 kHz for PQ measurement indoor voltage transformer narrow design. The voltage transformers are manufactured in full encapsulation with casting resin, i.e. high voltage, secondary winding(s) and core are encapsulated in one process. The GSES 12D / 24D with optional extended frequency range is optimized for future standard requirements and measurement tasks up to 9 kHz, i.e. there is no resonance point up to this frequency. The proven transformer principle is used. The voltage transformers are designed for use in 20 kV medium-voltage networks..

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
