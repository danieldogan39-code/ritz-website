Áttekintés

GSWS 12, GSWS 24 | 12 | 17,5 | 24 | Indoor support current transformers, block type, narrow design, DIN 42600, part 8 are cast resin insulated. The current transformers are fully encapsulated with brown epoxy resin, i.e. high voltage winding, secondary winding(s) and core(s) are encapsulated in one process. For a voltage display or simple voltage measurement, a capacitive measuring layer can be provided, which is led to a terminal in the terminal box. - Primarily switchable transformers are always supplied in parallel (for the high current strength). Any mounting position is possible. Grounding: The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear. The secondary terminals are housed in a terminal box which can be sealed in the case of revenue measurement.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
