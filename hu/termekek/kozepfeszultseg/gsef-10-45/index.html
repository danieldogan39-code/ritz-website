Áttekintés

GSEF 10…45 Outdoor voltage transformer, single-pole insulated.

The outdoor voltage transformers are insulated with cast resin. Primary winding and core with secondary winding are cast in one operation with cycloaliphatic epoxy resin prepared under vacuum (full encapsulation). The secondary terminals are housed in a sealable terminal box. The secondary connection is made of tinned brass. The primary connection consists of a hexagon head screw. The mounting position is upright. The voltage transformer is grounded at the rear of the transformer. The colour of the casting body is brown, also available in ANSI grey at an extra charge.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
