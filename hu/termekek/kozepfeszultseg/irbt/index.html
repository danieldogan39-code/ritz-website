Áttekintés

IRBT Indoor Generator Window Type Current Transformer. The transformers are suitable for measurement and protection purposes and meet the requirements for the extended current measurement range. The terminal box cover can be sealed to prevent tampering. The secondary terminal box can be sealed. For measuring and protection equipment. Concretely realizable rated powers of the individual cores depend on the number of cores, class accuracy, rated current and overcurrent number.

Újdonság

Optimised design: rated for ambient temperatures up to 80 °C, approx. 15 % lower height and 5–10 % less weight; additional terminal box for improved secondary connection. For currents up to 50,000 A; compensating windings cancel external-field effects at small phase spacings.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
