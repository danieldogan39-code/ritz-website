Áttekintés

Outdoor head-type current transformer, also available to GOST

GIF 12 | 17,5 | 24 | 36 | 52 | 72,5 | Outdoor Support Type Current Transformers, Head-Type, are cast resin insulated. Primary winding as well as the core(s) with secondary winding(s) are moulded in one single process with epoxy resin, which has been processed under vacuum. The secondary terminals are located in a sturdy terminal box made of cast aluminium. For metering transformers this terminal box can also be sealed. Primary terminals are optionally inserts, bolts terminal pads or clamp type connectors. Routine Tests: The routine tests include ratio-error and phase-angle-tests, induced and applied voltage tests and partial discharge tests. These tests are done in accordance with DIN EN 61869-2 (except when other standards have been agreed upon).

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
