Áttekintés

Voltage transformer, single-pole, large/support type to DIN 42600 part 3

VEN 12|17,5|24|36|52|72,5 Indoor voltage transformers of big design. The voltage transformers are fully encapsulated, i.e. high voltage, secondary winding(s) and core are encapsulated in a single process. The insulation consists of epoxy resin and forms at the same time the moulded body including the terminal box. The installation position is upright. Information on minimum distances between the phases and between earthed system components cannot be made generally binding, as these depend to a large extent on the installation conditions. The transformer can be earthed via the earthing screw at the rear or directly by connecting the base plate to the earthed frame of the switchgear.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
