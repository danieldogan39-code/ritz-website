Áttekintés

eA10A Explosion-proof Support-Type Current transformer

Purpose:

Installation in terminal boxes of electrical apparatus under potentially explosive gas atmospheres Minimum IP-code for enclosure: IP 54 acc. to EN 60529:1991

Installation:

Installation must be made in compliance with EN 60079-0:2012+A11:2013 and EN 600079-7:2007! The permissible creepage and air distances for material group I (CTI-Value > 600) must be strictly adhered.

Grounding:

The grounding must be done by using the grounding screw M 8 located at the backside of the CT. The minimum cross section of the grounding connection according to EN 60079-0:2012+A11:2013 must by adhered. The secondary winding has to be grounded on one side in the terminal box by the provided connection lug.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
