Áttekintés

KDR are Tube type current Transformer with eccentrically off-centered passage.

The primary conductor is a removable copper insert with a milled groove at the side for bolts M12 (M10 or M16)).

The insert overlaps the transformer housing on both sides and can therefore absorb the contact pressure generated during the screw connection.

possible design options

Two housing versions are available:

version a: aperture offset off-line

version b: aperture offset in-line

Size: W/H/D 54,5 / 75* / 32 mm

* (incl. terminal cover)

Weight: ≤ 0.25 kg

standard accessories:

transparent terminal cover

Mounting in any position

The current transformer is maintenance-free
