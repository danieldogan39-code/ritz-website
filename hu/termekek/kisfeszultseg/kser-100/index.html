KSER 100 | 0.72 | 1.2 | kV low voltage indoor voltage transformer, single pole.

Dimensions W/H/L: 100 / 126 / 70 mm

Weight: approx. 1.1 kg

Standard accessories:

1 transparent terminal cover

4 mounting feet

Possible special versions:

different rated frequency

tropical version

cast resin filling
