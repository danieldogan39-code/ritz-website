Áttekintés

(E) KSO 62 Indoor window type current transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

(E) KSS 62 bus bar current transformer with primary conductor

possible design options:

(E) KSO 62, inner diameter 20 mm without bus bar, 25 … 600A for bus bars up to 20 x 10 mm or round cables up to Ø 20 mm

(E) KSS 62, inner diameter 20 mm with bus bar, 25 … 600A for bus bars 20 x 10 mm,

Outer dimensions W / H / L

Size 1: 60 / 82 / 30 mm,

Size 2: 60 / 86 / 52 mm,

Size 3: 60 / 82 / 69 mm

Optional accessories:

Twistable snap-on mounting bracket for mounting rail (DIN EN 60715-TH35)

Bus bars

Um 1,2 kV (on request)

Standard accessories:

fixing device for primary conductor,

2 mounting feet,

transparent terminal cover

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.
