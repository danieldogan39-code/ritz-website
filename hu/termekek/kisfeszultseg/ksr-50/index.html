Áttekintés

Der KSR 50 is a Tube type ct with a secondary winding mounted on a ring coreThe active part is supported by two ultrasonically welded polycarbonate half-shells completely enclosed. The transformer is equipped with a removable primary conductor in the form of a copper tube.

possible model versions

Inner Ø 10 mm for bolts M10 up to Ø 10 mm

Size 1: W / H / D: 50 / 70 / 30 mm

Size 2: W / H / D: 50 / 70 / 50 mm

Size 3: W / H / D: 50 / 70 / 69 mm

Weight: 0.6 kg

Standard accessories:

2 mounting feet,

transparent terminal cover

Optional accessories:

Twistable snap-on mounting bracket for mounting rail (DIN EN 60715-TH35);

Mounting in any position. The current transformer is maintenance-free
