Áttekintés

ZKSW 800 Indoor Interposing Current Transformer

Rated Primary Current: 0,1…20 A

Size (W/H/L): 185 / 191 / variable mm

Standard Accessories:

4 Foot Mountings,

2 transparent terminal cover

Installation in any position.

Application

1. at lower operating currents, when no direct measurement is desired (galvanic separation of the measuring circuit, keeping the operating voltage away, nominal current of measuring instruments is not equal to the operating current). 2. to connect to the main transformer for the following purposes:

a) to reduce the transmission losses in long measuring lines: Reduce from e.g. 5 to 1A at the place of installation of the main transformer and increase from 1 to 5A at the place of measurement if necessary.

b) For the protection of sensitive equipment against overcurrents by means of a small overcurrent-limiting factor with the most accurate selection of the actually required rated burden.

c) To adapt the secondary current of the main transformer to the nominal current of the devices to be connected.

A termékadatok angol és német nyelven érhetők el.

Jellemzők

  • Beltéri
  • Tetszőleges beépítési helyzet
  • Egyfázisú
  • Környezeti hőmérséklet −5 … +40 °C
