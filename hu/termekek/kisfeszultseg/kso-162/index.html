Áttekintés

KSO 162 Indoor: Window Type Current Transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

possible model options

Inside-Opening; 52 mm for bus bars up to 2x60x10 (1x40x10) mm or for round cables up to Ø 52 mm

Size W/H/D: 100 / 126 / 62 mm;

Special designs:

Um 1.2 kV Accessories:

fixing device for primary conductor

transparent terminal cover

4 Foot mountings
