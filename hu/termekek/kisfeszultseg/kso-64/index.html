Áttekintés

(E) KSO 64 Indoor window type current transformer without primary conductor, standard series,

(E) KSS 64 busbar current transformer with primary conductor.

possible design options:

(E) KSO 64, inner diameter 40.3 mm without busbar, for busbars up to 40 x 10 mm or round cables up to Ø 17 mm

(E) KSS 64, inner diameter 40.3 mm with bus bar 40 x 10 mm,

Outer dimensions W / H / L

Size 1: 60 / 82 / 30 mm,

Size 2: 60 / 82 / 50 mm,

Size 3: 60 / 82 / 69 mm

Standard accessories:

fixing device for primary conductor,

2 mounting feet,

transparent terminal cover

 Bus bars
