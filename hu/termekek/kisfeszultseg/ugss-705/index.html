Áttekintés

UGSS 705; Split-core type current transformer, also for earth fault detection, for 12…36 kV.

These split-core ct’s are used to detect small earth currents. The retrofitting can be carried out retrospectively around already installed cables with fitted terminations. All three conductors of the cable are enclosed together. The earth wire is fed back on the cable within the transformer aperture. Due to the cable insulation, the transformer is independent of the operating voltage (application mainly for Um 12 kV to Um 36 kV).
